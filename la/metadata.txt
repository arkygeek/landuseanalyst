about=Models the agricultural catchment of an archaeological site. A diet/herd model turns population, diet composition and crop/animal profiles into land-area targets; a walking-time (Tobler) cost surface and a binary-search threshold sweep then locate that land around the settlement on a DEM with per-item suitability and slope limits. Dockable workspace with live diet preview, results tiles/charts/tables, PDF/JSON export. Python port of the C++ Landuse Analyst used in Jorgenson (2010); shares its engine with la.arkygeek.com.

tracker=https://github.com/arkygeek/landuseanalyst/issues
